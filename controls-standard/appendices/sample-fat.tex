\chapter{Sample FAT Document}
\label{app:sample-fat}
This appendix is a \textbf{sample} Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) document, laid out as a template with a few example rows completed for project \texttt{014\_R23-Column-PLC}. It is the ``FAT Checkout document'' of Chapter~\ref{ch:design-process}: outlined in Stage~4, procedures and expected results completed by late Stage~5, and actual results recorded in Stage~5A. The structure follows IEC 62381, and the electrical tests follow the testing and verification chapter of NFPA 79.\footnote{IEC 62381:2024 (Ed.~3), \emph{Automation systems in the process industry --- Factory acceptance test (FAT), site acceptance test (SAT), and site integration test (SIT)}; NFPA 79-2024, Ch.~18 (18.2 continuity of the effective ground-fault current path, 18.3 insulation resistance, 18.4 voltage tests, 18.6 functional tests); IEC 60204-1:2016+A1:2021, Cl.~18; UL 508A, 3rd ed. (panel construction and markings).} Remove the examples and adapt the sections to each project.

\section{Scope, References, and Test Setup}
\textbf{Scope:} the CP-1 control panel, its PLC program, and the SCADA screens for the R23 column, tested at the panel builder's shop before shipment. Field devices are simulated; tests that need the field are listed in Section~\ref{sec:fat-open} for commissioning (SAT).

\textbf{References} (record the revision used): electrical schematics and panel layout; I/O list; Control Narrative; Table of Interlocks and Permissives; alarm list; network map and address list.

\textbf{Test setup:} how field signals are simulated (loop calibrator, switches, PLC simulation), the SCADA computer used, and the test equipment with calibration dates (multimeter, insulation tester, loop calibrator).
