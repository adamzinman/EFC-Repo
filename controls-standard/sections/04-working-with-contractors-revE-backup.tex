\chapter{Working with Contractors}
\section{Scope and Responsibility}
The purchase specification must identify the contractor's design responsibility, applicable codes and editions, required certifications, supplied hardware/software, interfaces, exclusions, schedule, review milestones, testing, commissioning support, training, warranty, and turnover deliverables. Acceptance by EFC does not relieve the contractor of code compliance or design responsibility.

\section{Required Submittals}
Before fabrication or programming, contractors must submit the basis of design, component data, schematics, panel layout, SCCR calculation, enclosure heat calculation, I/O list, network architecture, software design, alarm list, bill of material, and FAT procedure. Deviations from this standard must be clearly listed; silence does not constitute approval.

\section{Panel Fabrication}
The purchase order must state whether a UL Listed industrial control panel is required. When listing is not required, panels must still follow the documented UL 508A design approach and this standard, but drawings and nameplates must not imply a UL Listing that does not exist. Fabricators must be qualified for the voltage, SCCR, environmental, and hazardous-location requirements of the project.

\section{Testing and Witnessing}
FAT must verify construction, drawing conformance, I/O, sequence logic, permissives, trips, alarms, communications, power cycling, loss of utilities, fail-safe behavior, security roles, backups, and recovery. Safety-related functions must be validated against an approved test specification. EFC must identify witness and hold points before fabrication.

\section{Turnover}
Turnover must include native editable source files, compiled/runtime files, firmware and software versions, device configuration files, certificates, license information, approved backups, credential transfer to the approved vault, test records, calibration records, redlines/as-builts, spare-parts list, training material, and known open items. Contractor accounts and remote access must be removed or explicitly transferred at closeout.
